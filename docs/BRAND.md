# Tivzo identity

Name: Tivzo (tiv-zoh). A coined name with no established dictionary meaning.

The mark is a compact, forward-leaning T cut out of a rounded square, with a small orange counterform. It works as a monochrome brand shape and a favicon. Wordmark: lowercase tivzo with an orange full stop.

Direction: crisp editorial typography, clear spacing, flat color, restrained borders. No ambient lighting, glowing borders, glass effects or background gradients. Light surfaces use white/cool grey; dark surfaces use charcoal. Orange marks primary context and progress. The event cover treatments are editable typography and solid color, not externally sourced photographs or copies of another brand.

This name passed an initial web search for obvious app/business use. Domain and trademark clearance have not been established.
